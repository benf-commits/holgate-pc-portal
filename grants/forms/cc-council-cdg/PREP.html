⚠️ Eligibility blocker — read first

The guidelines list "works to building or property" (4.4.23) and existing programs needing more funding (4.4.20) as INELIGIBLE. Our canteen shutters/asbestos and the playground floor are capital/building works → not eligible under this grant. CDG funds programs, events, community participation, capacity building and social-inclusion activities — not infrastructure. - Other constraints: project cannot start before 1 Sep 2026, must end by 30 Apr 2028; decisions at the 27 Oct 2026 Council meeting; public liability min $20M (note: higher than our standard $5M — CONFIRM cover).

The factual/org fields below are pre-filled. The project-narrative fields require an eligible (non-capital) project — none currently in scope. Do not submit for shutters/playground.
