Holgate Public School P&C Association

Grant Applications

This document covers the four grants in scope. For each one it sets out the current status, where to apply, the official forms, and the responses we have prepared.

All four grants are submitted through online portals, so there are no fillable PDF forms to complete. Each prepared-responses file answers that form's actual question set, so the content is ready to paste straight into the portal. Last updated 1 June 2026.

Community Building Partnership (CBP) 2026

Detailed app opens 29 Jun (invite-only)
Project
Canteen shutter replacement + functional upgrade
Amount
$10,000 – $100,000
Closes
17 July 2026, 5pm (Detailed Application)
Apply
https://communitybuildingpartnership.smartygrants.com.au/ - invite-only for shortlisted EOI applicants
Enquiries
cbp2026@dcj.nsw.gov.au · (02) 8753 8144
Eligibility
P&C explicitly eligible · capital works eligible · DoE AMU portal approval (PDF) required for school land
This is the only one of the four grants that suits a capital project like the canteen shutters. The Detailed Application is open to shortlisted applicants only, so it depends on whether an Expression of Interest was lodged between 20 April and 8 May 2026. Confirm that with Adam Crouch's office on (02) 4365 1906 before relying on this round. The grant covers the shutter replacement only, because asbestos removal is the Department of Education's responsibility rather than the P&C's.

ClubGRANTS Category 2 - Gosford RSL & Davistown RSL

Open now
Project
Modular sports surface (playground floor, or a basketball-court component)
Amount
Club discretion
Closes
Gosford 30 Jun 2026 · Davistown 17 Jul 2026
Apply
https://www.clubgrants.com.au - register, then Gosford round 574986 / Davistown round 475271
Eligibility
P&Cs/schools explicitly eligible (Cat 2). Gosford requires the 2025 Funding Report lodged first if previously funded.
ClubGRANTS Category 2 has no application form to download. You apply through the online portal, and the exact questions, including any a club adds itself, only appear once you register and open the round. Our prepared responses cover the mandatory structure and the criteria the clubs use to assess applications.

Central Coast Council - Community Development Grant 2026-27

Capital works ineligible
Amount
Up to $20,000
Closes
30 June 2026
Apply
https://centralcoast.smartygrants.com.au/CDG2627 (online only)
Enquiries
grants@centralcoast.nsw.gov.au · (02) 4325 8861
Constraints
Project cannot start before 1 Sep 2026 · public liability minimum $20M · decided 27 Oct 2026
This grant does not fit our current projects. The guidelines rule out works to a building or property under clause 4.4.23, which excludes both the canteen shutters and the playground floor. The program funds community programs, events and participation activities rather than infrastructure. The prepared responses fill in the organisation and governance fields, but the project narrative needs an eligible non-capital project before this can be submitted.

Stronger Communities Programme - Round 10

Not open yet
Project
Playground modular floor ($20k grant + $10k school) · Starlink also eligible
Amount
Grant $2,500 – $20,000 · project max $50,000
Status
Budget-funded 12 May 2026; guidelines not yet released. R9 form below shows the question set.
Apply
MP-nominated - contact Emma McBride (Member for Dobell), emmamcbride.com.au, then complete the online portal via the invitation link
This program is nominated by your federal MP, so you cannot apply directly. The first step is to ask Emma McBride, the Member for Dobell, to nominate the project, and the online application only opens after that. Do not approach the Minister or the department yourself, because the nomination is the MP's role. Round 10 guidelines are not out yet, so the prepared responses follow the Round 9 form, which may change slightly.
