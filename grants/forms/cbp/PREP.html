Criterion 1 — Strengthen community wellbeing

The canteen is the only weekday food point and the de facto community hub for Holgate/Matcham, a semi-rural area with no shops, no public transport and no public meeting spaces within walking distance. It is the kitchen and service point for every major community event the school runs — Election Day (a NSW polling place; 200+ served in 2025), Father's/Mother's Day breakfasts, Cross Country, Trivia Nights and discos. The existing roller shutters are non-functional and cannot be secured. New shutters and a functional service-window upgrade make the canteen safe, weatherproof and lockable, directly improving an inclusive and accessible community facility used by the whole community.

Benefits: a secure, all-weather service point for community events; improved food security (only accessible weekday food option); safer conditions for the 100% volunteer workforce; capacity for larger and evening events.

Groups who benefit: ~125 students and their families; the broader Holgate/Matcham community at events; volunteers.

Evidence: Election Day canteen served 200+ community members (2025); canteen operates ~$550/week; ~17 active P&C volunteers. CONFIRM any survey/feedback data.
