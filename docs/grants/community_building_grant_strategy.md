## Draft Narrative Responses

### Q38: How will your project enhance facilities for the local community?

- The school canteen is the primary food service facility for all major community events in the Holgate/Matcham valley, including Election Day BBQs, Father's Day Breakfasts, Cross Country carnivals, Trivia Nights, and school discos
- Holgate/Matcham is a semi-rural, isolated community with no local shops or public gathering spaces — the school is the de facto community hub
- Current asbestos contamination prevents any modifications, meaning we cannot install proper kitchen exhaust/ventilation, replace unsafe non-functional shutters, or relocate electrical fixtures for improved workflow
- Upgraded shutters will create a safer, more efficient service point for large community events (Election Day 2025 served 200+ community members)
- Removing the asbestos hazard allows future improvements including potential commercial kitchen certification for expanded community use
- The canteen services not just school families but the broader community during polling days, community fundraisers, and school open days

### Q39: How will your project meet community needs?

- Food security: The canteen is the only accessible weekday food option for students — nearest shops are a 10-minute drive away in Erina, with no public transport available
- Volunteer safety: Parent volunteers currently work in a facility with identified asbestos hazards; remediation addresses a direct WHS concern
- Affordable meals: The canteen provides low-cost meal options supporting families experiencing financial hardship in our community
- Event capability: Major P&C fundraising events (which fund school programs, equipment, and facilities) depend on a functional canteen — current state limits what we can safely deliver
- The non-functional shutters are a security risk (cannot be properly locked) and prevent efficient service during high-volume events
- Proper ventilation (impossible with asbestos walls) would allow expanded hot food preparation, meeting parent demand for healthier, freshly-cooked options

### Q40: How will your project increase community participation?

- Volunteer recruitment: Asbestos presence actively deters parent volunteers — several prospective helpers have declined due to safety concerns; remediation removes this barrier
- The canteen is 100% volunteer-run by P&C parents; improved facilities directly increase volunteer satisfaction and retention
- Year 6 students participate in canteen operations as part of the school's leadership program — upgraded facilities enhance this intergenerational learning opportunity
- Enables expanded programming: cooking/nutrition workshops for parents and students, cultural food days celebrating our diverse community (Harmony Day events)
- Functional evening service capability (proper lighting, ventilation) allows more community events like Trivia Nights and school discos
- Creates a showcase facility that supports HeadStart kindy transition program and school tours — first impressions matter for new family engagement
- Better facilities = more successful events = more funds raised = more community benefit (virtuous cycle)

### Q41: What capacity does your organisation have to deliver the proposed project?

- Established Grants Committee with dedicated members (Vice President, Grants Coordinator, and committee member) experienced in grant applications and acquittals
- Strong financial governance: dedicated Treasurer, annual independent audit, compliant with NSW P&C Federation requirements, dual-signatory controls on all accounts
- Existing relationship with NSW Department of Education ASSETS team who have already scoped and quoted the work
- Demonstrated project delivery capability through ongoing school improvement initiatives (recent examples: LED signage, PBL signage, asphalt works, basketball court grant application)
- Active executive committee of 4 office bearers plus 13 additional P&C members across fundraising, grants, canteen, uniform, and communications roles
- Principal actively engaged in P&C activities and committed to supporting the project (available for co-sign/endorsement)
- Committed co-funding: Gittoes Real Estate has pledged $2,000 donation; school has indicated capacity to contribute from operational budget
- Clear project scope already defined through ASSETS consultation — ready to proceed immediately upon funding approval
