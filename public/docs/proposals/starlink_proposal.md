# Proposal: Starlink Mini Connectivity for P&C Events

**Submitted by:** Dave Gurr, Vice President
**Date:** 1 December 2025
**For consideration at:** December 2025 P&C Meeting

---

## Background

Mobile coverage in Holgate remains extremely limited, with the Telstra tower not expected until 2026. The school has no accessible internet for P&C activities, which creates ongoing challenges for our events and canteen operations.

## The Problem

At present, we struggle with:

- Processing EFTPOS and Spriggy payments reliably at events (election day BBQs, Mother's/Father's Day stalls, trivia nights, discos)
- Volunteer coordination during events
- Emergency communications when issues arise
- Real-time logistics management
- Canteen payment processing on Fridays

## Proposed Solution

Dave Gurr has a portable Starlink Mini unit that can provide reliable, high-speed internet connectivity for P&C activities. The proposal is to fund a 12-month trial of the service for P&C use.

**What the Starlink Mini provides:**

- Portable satellite internet (300+ Mbps speeds)
- Works anywhere with clear sky view
- Compact and easy to set up at events
- Can support multiple devices simultaneously

**Intended use:**

- All P&C events requiring payment processing or coordination
- Canteen operations (Spriggy payments)
- Emergency communications backup
- P&C meeting connectivity when needed

This would be for P&C operational use only, not a general public wifi service.

## Cost

**Annual service cost:** A$480 (A$40/month on the Roam 50GB plan)

The hardware is already owned and available at no cost to the P&C.

## Funding Approach

Rather than using P&C funds, we propose seeking a local business sponsor to cover the annual service cost. In return, the sponsor would receive:

- A small sign acknowledging their support displayed at any P&C event where we're using the connectivity
- Acknowledgment in the school newsletter and P&C Facebook page

If this proposal is approved, a simple sponsorship proposal will be prepared to approach potential sponsors.

## Financial Arrangement

The service would be attached to Dave's existing Starlink account for simplicity. The P&C would receive the sponsorship funds and reimburse Dave as a one-off annual payment.

## Recommendation

That the P&C:

1. Approve a 12-month trial of Starlink Mini connectivity for P&C events and canteen operations
2. Approve seeking a business sponsor to cover the A$480 annual cost
3. Authorise the Vice President to approach potential sponsors with a sponsorship proposal

---

**Motion:**
That the P&C approve the Starlink Mini connectivity proposal as a 12-month trial, funded through business sponsorship, with the sponsorship proposal to be circulated to potential sponsors upon approval.
